Enrolment Process

We accept enrolment enquiries throughout the school year. Our Assistant Principal looks after every enrolment, with a small panel helping to make each decision.

  1. 1

    Make an enquiry

    Phone the College on 03 5831 4029 to ask about places in your child’s year level. If the year level is full, your child can join a waitlist.

  2. 2

    Book a school tour

    Visit with your child to see the school and talk through the curriculum. Bring copies of your child’s most recent reports, or email them to us, along with their most recent disability report if this applies.

  3. 3

    Enrolment Assessment Form

    Your child’s current school completes the Enrolment Assessment Form and emails it to us. If your child isn’t currently at a school, you can complete it and return it to the office. We may also ask for an interview.

  4. 4

    Hear back from us

    The panel considers your child’s age, year level and the support we can provide. We’ll let you know our decision within a week of receiving all the information.

  5. 5

    Confirm your place

    With your offer you’ll receive the enrolment forms. Return them within five school days with a $200 deposit to confirm your child’s place. We’ll take copies of their birth certificate, Medicare card and concession card, if you have one.

    Decided not to accept? Just call us on 03 5831 4029.
