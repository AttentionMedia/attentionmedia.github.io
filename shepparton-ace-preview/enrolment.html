How to enrol

Three simple steps to get started.

See the full process →
  1. 1

    Call us

    Phone 03 5831 4029 to check places in your child’s year level.

  2. 2

    Take a tour

    Visit with your child, meet us and talk through the curriculum.

  3. 3

    Hear back in a week

    Once we have everything we need, we’ll let you know within a week.

What to bring

Bring these to your tour.

  • Your child’s most recent school reports
  • Their most recent disability report, if this applies
  • Year 7: your primary school completes an Enrolment Assessment Form and sends it to us
