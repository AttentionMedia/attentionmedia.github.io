About ACE

A small school, by choice.

Shepparton ACE Secondary College is a small, very low fee, independent secondary school in the heart of Shepparton. We offer secondary education and vocational courses in a relaxed, community-based setting where teachers and students work together.

Our story

Twenty years of small classes.

ACE started out about 20 years ago as a senior school. Since 2019 we have taught Years 7 to 12, and we intend to stay small, with classes capped at 15 students.

Students follow the Australian Curriculum in Years 7 to 10, then choose VCE, VCE VM or VET in Years 11 and 12. Camps and incursions are cost-free.
