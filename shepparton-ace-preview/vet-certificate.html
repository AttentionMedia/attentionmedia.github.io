Certificate II in Cookery

About the course

This qualification covers kitchen operations and defined food preparation skills, mostly routine tasks carried out under direct supervision.

It doesn’t meet the requirements for trade recognition as a cook, but it can be a pathway towards it.

Where it can lead

  • Restaurants
  • Hotels
  • Catering
  • Clubs and pubs
  • Cafes
  • Aged care
  • Hospitals
  • Schools
