What is ChatGPT Advertising?

A brand-new channel, still in beta.

How the ads appear

One clearly-labelled unit, below the answer.

When someone's conversation is relevant to what you offer, a single sponsored unit can appear beneath ChatGPT's response, clearly labelled and visually separate. It shows your business name, logo, a headline, short copy and a link straight to your landing page. Ads never change ChatGPT's actual answers, and they only appear on the Free and Go plans.

Step by step

Setting up your ChatGPT Ads account.

The self-serve platform lives at ads.openai.com. These screenshots are from our own account setup, so this is the exact process we went through.

Step 1

Start your account setup

Sign up at ads.openai.com using an OpenAI account. The onboarding walks you through four stages: business details, account details, verification, and account review.

Step 2

Confirm your account details

Set your country, currency and timezone. For us that's Australia, AUD and Melbourne time. Take care here: these settings are locked once the account is created. And if you work with an agency like us, note the advertiser creates their own account first, then adds the agency's team to it.

Step 3

Verify your business

Verification runs through Persona, OpenAI's identity partner. It takes a few minutes and checks your business is eligible to advertise under OpenAI's advertising policy.

Step 4

Wait out the review queue

Your application then sits in review. OpenAI is upfront that signup volume is high and verification can take some time, which is another reason to get your account in early, before you need it.

Step 5

Access granted

Once approved, the welcome email lands and you can log in to Ads Manager Beta. OpenAI's own help articles are worth a read at this point, or you can skip ahead and let us handle it.

Step 6

Build your first campaign

Add billing (campaigns run from A$25 a day), choose an objective of Reach, Clicks or Conversions, set your budget and locations, then describe in plain language the conversations where your ads belong. No keyword lists. OpenAI notes delivery ramps gradually during the beta, so expect a measured start.

Common questions

ChatGPT Advertising FAQs.

How much does ChatGPT advertising cost?
Campaigns start from a minimum daily budget of A$25. You pay per click through a second-price auction, and OpenAI suggests starting bids of around US$3 to US$5 per click. There's no big upfront commitment, which makes it a low-risk channel to test.
Who actually sees ads in ChatGPT?
Ads appear for logged-in adults on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans, which cover the vast majority of everyday users. Paid Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise subscribers don't see ads, and neither do users under 18. Australian users are included in the rollout.
Will ChatGPT recommend my business in its answers?
No. Ads sit in a clearly labelled sponsored unit below the response and never influence what ChatGPT actually says. That separation is a core principle of OpenAI's ads program, and it's what keeps the platform trustworthy for users.
How does targeting work without keywords?
Instead of bidding on keywords, you describe in plain language the kinds of conversations where your ads belong, and OpenAI's systems match your ads to relevant chats. You can also target by location and upload customer lists as custom audiences. There's no demographic or interest targeting like Meta.
Can I measure leads and sales from ChatGPT Ads?
Yes. OpenAI provides a website pixel and a server-side Conversions API, so you can track form submissions, purchases and other actions back to your ads. Campaigns using the Conversions objective can optimise delivery toward those events, and your usual UTM tracking carries through on every click.
Is my business eligible to advertise?
Most consumer-facing businesses are: household goods, local and trade services, travel, hospitality, education and digital products. Some categories can't advertise yet, including financial services and healthcare (outside the US), alcohol, gambling, legal services and political content. Every advertiser also goes through business verification before ads can run. If you're unsure where you land, ask us on a discovery call.
